# Requirements Change Log (SRS → Design)

Part II design document — DecisionTrace

**Status:** Current (2026-11-19) — C26 is the repositioning entry; the **Standing** column says what each C1–C25 entry means for the current system.
**Baseline:** `docs/SRS/srs.md` (submitted 2026-10-23)

Every requirement change between the SRS baseline and the Part II design,
with its reason and evidence. The SRS itself stays untouched: this log is
the authoritative record of how the design evolved. Entries superseded by
C26 stay here as the historical record — they are not a description of
the current system; the current system is the one C26 defines.

| C20 | Critical path analysis (planned, Part III) | SRS: topological order of the dependency graph only | Earliest/latest scheduling over the graph with due dates, surfacing the commitments actually on the critical path | Part III plan — unaffected by the repositioning | Topological insight upgrade | `docs/design/references.md` (Cormen) |
| C21 | Decision pre-check gate before confirmation | Not in SRS | Confirming a decision candidate first drafted counter-considerations from real sources; each needed a resolution reason, recorded as resolved `AGAINST` arguments | Superseded by C26 — removed | A ledger records what was said; an analyst also surfaces what was not addressed | PR #30 |
| C22 | Reversal trace for overturned decisions | SRS: decisions carry a status; no link between a decision and its replacement | `SUPERSEDED` could name the successor (`superseded_by_decision_id`); `GET /decisions/{id}/reversal` assembled the narrative | Superseded by C26 — the chain itself is the revision relationship; the `superseded_by` column stays only for pre-repositioning rows | "Why did this decision die?" is the most informative question about a decision | PR #30 |
